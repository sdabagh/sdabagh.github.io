Teaching Philosophy

Statistics is one of the most powerful tools a citizen can hold, and mathematics education belongs to every student, not to a few. My practice rests on three commitments: access (free, advanced materials and a free statistical app, so no student is held back by cost), representation (open materials that show students who does statistics), and students as creators of knowledge. I see the classroom as the place where students discover themselves as leaders. Read my full statement.
