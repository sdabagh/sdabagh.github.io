Start here.
  1. Data: open a CSV from your computer, paste from a spreadsheet, or pick a sample dataset.
  2. Summary and Graph: pick a column, read the numbers, see the picture.
  3. Inference: intervals and tests. Every result gives the statistic, the p-value, and the sentence to write.
  4. Calculators: binomial, normal, and t, with the picture shaded.
Means always use t with degrees of freedom (the population standard deviation is never given). Decisions use the p-value approach. There is no inference about a variance in this course, so there is none here.
