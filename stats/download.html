The installable app is coming soon

Stats Without Walls will be offered as an app for phones, tablets and computers once every part of it has been checked in real classes. Until then it is not available to install, and the earlier desktop snapshot has been withdrawn, so that nobody keeps an old copy that no longer matches the lessons. Whether the app stays free for everyone, or is free for students with a small fee for researchers, has not been decided yet; the browser version is free.
