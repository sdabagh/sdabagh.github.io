Stats Without Walls

Open the web version   learnwithoutwalls.com

Free statistics for the intro classroom. Three ways to run it; all three are the same program with the same menus.

1. In your browser (nothing to install)

Open Stats Without Walls

Works on any computer, Chromebook, tablet or phone. After the first visit it keeps working without internet. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Install it from the browser (offline, one click)

Open the web version, then: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the three-dot menu, then Install Stats Without Walls. In Safari on a Mac, File, then Add to Dock. It then opens like any app and works offline.

3. Download the desktop app (offline, no browser)

Mac, Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) Mac, Intel Windows

About 100 MB each. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu, About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M..." means Apple Silicon, "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

Mac, first open: open the .dmg, drag the app to Applications. Because the app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate, the first time you open it right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open, then Open again in the dialog. After that it opens normally.

Windows, first open: run the installer. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The app installs for your user and puts a shortcut on the desktop.

Version 1.0.0, September 2026. The web version always has the newest features; the desktop app is updated a few times a term.

Questions or something not working: email your instructor with what you clicked and what you saw.
