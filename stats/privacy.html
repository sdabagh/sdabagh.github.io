Stats Without Walls: privacy

Open the app   User guide   learnwithoutwalls.com

Short version. Stats Without Walls has no server and no account. The data you open, type or paste, and every result, stay on your device. The developer receives nothing: no data, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no email address.

What the app stores on your device

The app runs entirely in your browser (or in the installed app, which is the same program). Your browser's local storage keeps, on that device only:

  • Your own data, with its filter and the variable types you set by hand: a file you opened, data you pasted, typed or simulated, and a sample dataset once you change it. The bar above the table says "remembered from your last visit" when this is what you see. Data › New data table, or opening another file, replaces it.
  • The choices behind your last results, so the app can offer to run them again after a reload. Nothing is run until you accept.
  • Settings: student or researcher mode and your field, decimal places, notation, interface language, the accessibility options (dark mode, large text, reading font) and the course profile if an instructor gave you one.
  • The app's own files, cached by a service worker so that it opens and works without internet after the first visit.

Clearing the site's data in your browser settings removes all of it. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing from your data or results. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party script that sees the page. Analyses, graphs and the Ask box are computed locally.
  • The app's files and its sample datasets are fetched from this site when it loads or updates.
  • If you turn on the reading font in the accessibility panel, the font file (Lexend) is fetched from Google Fonts; that request carries your IP address, as any download does, and no data from the app.
  • A share link or a saved session carries your setup, and the data table if it is small, only if you send the link or the file to someone. The app never sends either on its own.

Accounts, payments, children

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no payments inside the app. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children; a student can use it without giving a name or an email address.

The desktop download

The Mac and Windows apps on the download page are the same program packaged to run without a browser. They store the same things in the app's own data folder and send nothing.
