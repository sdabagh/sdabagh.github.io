Current Courses (Fall 2026)

This fall I teach introductory statistics at three campuses. Every section uses the free resources on this site: the statistics textbook, the Stats Without Walls tool, and the self paced lessons.

Math C1000: Introduction to Statistics

Institution: Santa Monica College

Sections: Two sections, one paired with the Math 54C support course

Math C1000 is the common course numbered statistics course (formerly Math 54). Topics include data collection, descriptive statistics, probability, distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, and chi square tests. Math 54C is a corequisite support course that reviews the algebra and reasoning skills you need to succeed in C1000.

Math 205: Applied Statistics in R

Institution: Loyola Marymount University

Sections: Two sections

A first course in statistical reasoning using R: data wrangling with the tidyverse, visualization, probability, inference, and regression, with examples drawn mainly from biology and the life sciences.
